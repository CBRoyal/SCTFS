How to Start a Survivor Fire

Did you know that a popular thing on so many Survivor fans' bucket list is starting fire the very first time on Survivor. Fire is essential in Survivor because in the game, fire represents your life in the game. Once your fire is gone, so are you! Fire is also essential at your tribe camp as well because you need it to stay warm at night and cook your rice, fruits, fish, and whatever items you earn as reward that you need to cook.

You might also need to be able to start fire on Survivor incase your at the final 4, and you are one of the two players fighting it out for the final spot in the final 3 at the fire-making challenge, and you need those fire-making skills to win. Well, this webpage will show how to start fire on Survivor.
How to Start a Fire with Flint and Machete
  1. Try to put the tip of the flint on the tinder of your campfire.
  2. Put the machete or steel on top of the flint and shave it down to get sparks.
  3. Once you have a fire, turn it to give it more fuel and air to grow higher.
  4. Use coconut husk, leaves, grass, or sticks to try to add fuel to your fire.
  5. Try to add more sticks on top in a teepee-style to let the fire catch it to add more fuel
* When your done with your campfire, make sure you put it out with water.
Now just a heads up. If your tribe looses an immunity challenge, not only will you be going to Tribal Council, but you will also lose your flint in the process and won't be able to win it back until your tribe wins a future immunity challenge. But it doesn't mean that you still can't start a fire, if you use a hand drill. All you have to do is use a 5-inch wide stick into the fire pit and rub the stick between your two fat palms whilst pressing down into the board until you get a fire going.
